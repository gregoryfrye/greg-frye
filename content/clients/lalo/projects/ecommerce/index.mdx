The homepage leads with the promise — simple, researched, well-made baby essentials — and goes straight to the Daily Stroller, "the fits-all, does-all" product the brand launched on.

The product page sells the system, not just the stroller: colorways, bundles with the bassinet, add-ons like the child tray and cup holder, and monthly payments for a high-ticket first purchase.

**Answers.** Can a newborn ride in it? Is it easy to fold? How long will it last? Answers sits in the main navigation, organized by durability, versatility, safety, convenience, and value — the research a parent would otherwise do elsewhere.
