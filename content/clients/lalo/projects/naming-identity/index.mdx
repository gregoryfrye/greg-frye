**Name.** We started by breaking down what Momo was doing right: short, two syllables at most, soft to say. Then I added one test: could a toddler say it? These are the products a child grows up with while learning to talk. "Can I get in my Lalo?" beats a stroller name no kid can pronounce. We built the shortlist from sounds small children make early, and Lalo fit the world we'd already designed.

**Wordmark.** Rounded geometric letters on a strict grid, built to run wide or stack two-over-two. A small animal mark rides alongside. Navy, coral, butter, and blush keep it warm without going pastel.
