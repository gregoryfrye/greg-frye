<SpineSection label="Context" subhead="A stroller start-up taking on industry giants">

Lalo launched direct-to-consumer into a category dominated by legacy brands, leading with one product: the Daily Stroller.

We named it, branded it, and built the store.

</SpineSection>

<SpineSection label="Complication" subhead="Three audiences, one first purchase">

New parents researching, babies who'd use the product, and grandparents who'd often pay for it. A baby gear purchase is high-stakes and heavily researched, and a new brand has no reputation to lean on.

Trust, a smooth path to purchase, and a system that could stretch as the line grew were the launch priorities.

</SpineSection>

<SpineSection label="Approach" subhead="Simple, researched, well-made — and answer every question">

The brand made one promise: fewer, better baby essentials. The site backed it with answers — a dedicated section for the questions parents ask before they buy, built into navigation alongside the product.

</SpineSection>
