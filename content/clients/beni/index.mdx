<SpineSection label="Context" subhead="A rug made to order, an ocean away">

Beni sells handwoven Moroccan rugs direct from weavers in the Atlas Mountains. Every rug is custom: the customer picks pattern, size, and color online, and a weaver makes it by hand.

I joined while the co-founders were still shaping the idea, with Rachel Yeager leading the account at Human NYC. We mapped the first digital experience together and helped choose the name. I designed the brand, the site and ecommerce, and the launch print.

</SpineSection>

<SpineSection label="Complication" subhead="Two users on opposite ends of one order">

A custom rug asks a lot of the buyer: commit to a product that doesn't exist yet, made by someone they'll never meet, and wait for it. The same order has to arrive at the loom as something a weaver can make.

The flow had to work for both — confidence for the customer online, clarity for the maker on the loom.

</SpineSection>

<SpineSection label="Approach" subhead="Go to the source">

I traveled with the two co-founders, a photographer, and a videographer: about two weeks, starting in Marrakesh, then up into the Atlas Mountains to meet the weavers.

The trip was brand research, product research, and production at once. Every photo and film the brand launched with came back from it.

</SpineSection>

<SpineSection label="Positioning" subhead="10,000 years of tradition — brought straight to your home">

The rug is the oldest thing in the room and the most personal. Beni sells both: a tradition with a long lineage, and a rug no one else has.

The aim was to bring the weavers' work to more people, on fair terms for the weavers themselves.

</SpineSection>

## Results

- Name, identity, site, customization flow, and launch print, from first conversation to launch
- All launch photography and film shot on one trip to Marrakesh and the Atlas Mountains
