<SpineSection label="Context" subhead="A rug made to order, an ocean away">

Beni sells handwoven Moroccan rugs direct from weavers in the Atlas Mountains. Every rug is custom: the customer picks pattern, size, and color online, and a weaver makes it by hand.

We took it from idea to launch — name, brand, site, and the customization flow.

</SpineSection>

<SpineSection label="Complication" subhead="Two users on opposite ends of one order">

A custom rug asks a lot of the buyer: commit to a product that doesn't exist yet, made by someone they'll never meet, and wait for it. The same order has to arrive at the loom as something a weaver can make.

The flow had to work for both — confidence for the customer online, clarity for the maker on the loom.

</SpineSection>

<SpineSection label="Approach" subhead="Go to the source">

We traveled to Morocco with the team — Marrakesh and the Atlas Mountains — to meet the weavers, see the process, and shoot the content the brand would launch on.

The trip did three jobs at once: brand inspiration, product research, and production. The identity, the copy, and the photography all came back from the same place the rugs come from.
