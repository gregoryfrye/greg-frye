The brand vocabulary came off the walls. Chalk marks, scratched geometry, and painted plaster in terracotta and rose sat next to the same diamond and zigzag patterns the weavers were knotting into wool.

We studied the process end to end — raw wool, spinning, the loom — and shot it as we went. The art direction stayed close to hands and material: no styled interiors, no models, just the work.
