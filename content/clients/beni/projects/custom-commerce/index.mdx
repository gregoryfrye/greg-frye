**Customize.** Pick your pattern, size, and colors, and Beni takes care of the rest. Each pattern is named and explained — Zag, "a simplified expression of traditional Berber geometry" — so a customer understands what they're choosing, not just how it looks. Size, wool, and color resolve into a live preview and a price.

On the other end, those same four choices are the spec. Nothing in the flow lets a customer order something a weaver can't make.
