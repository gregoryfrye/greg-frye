**Name.** Beni comes from the Beni Ourain, the weaving tradition these rugs are named for. It also sat comfortably among the first-name brands of the moment, friendly enough for direct-to-consumer. Playful on the surface, and an acknowledgment of the people who made the craft.

**Mark.** A single stacked glyph built from the diamond-and-zigzag geometry found in traditional Berber rugs. It reads as a pattern before it reads as a letter, and it holds at favicon size.

**Voice.** Plain and specific. "Made in Morocco." "Woven in the Atlas." "The new yarn is strung onto looms." The copy leads with place and process and lets the product carry the romance.
