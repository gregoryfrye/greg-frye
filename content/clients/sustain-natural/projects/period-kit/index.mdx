Most subscriptions sell a fixed box. Periods aren't fixed: products, absorbency, and length vary by person and by month.

So the kit starts with the buyer, in her own words. Fill-in-the-blank sentences replace a product grid: *I use tampons, pads, and liners, and on average my period lasts __ days. I use regular and super tampons, and I use about __ per day.* The answers build the box — tampons by absorbency, pads, liners — with quantities adjustable before checkout and a link for anyone whose flow doesn't fit the defaults.

The opening screen sets up why it matters: the FDA does not require feminine hygiene manufacturers to disclose ingredients. Sustain does.
