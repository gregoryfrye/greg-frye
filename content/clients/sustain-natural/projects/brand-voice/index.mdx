**Voice.** "Think with your vagina." "Your vagina matters." "Raise your standards." Short, direct, a little funny — and never at the buyer's expense. The headline earns the attention; the body copy does the education.

**Badges.** Claims became stickers: Organic, Fair Trade, pH Balanced, Triple Tested, Body Safe, Vagina Friendly — and No Means No. Each one is a specific promise the buyer can check, set in the brand's color system so they read as voice, not compliance.

**Our promise.** No nasty chemicals. No animal by-products. No parabens. No glycerin. No synthetic fragrances.
