<SpineSection label="Context" subhead="A male-dominated category with a proven primary purchaser">

Sustain made natural condoms, lube, and wipes, then organic tampons, pads, and liners. The sexual wellness aisle was built for men — clinical packaging, bro humor, or both. Yet women were more often the ones buying condoms for the home, and no condom brand spoke to them.

Sustain sold to that buyer directly.

</SpineSection>

<SpineSection label="Complication" subhead="The brief said loud. The first drafts whispered.">

The founder, Meika, wanted a brand that took up space: no hiding the purchase, and room to call out inequities like taxes on everyday women's health products. The brand had to say *vagina* on a shelf and on a homepage, and stay credible to a buyer who'd been ignored or talked down to.

The team's first drafts went discreet and quietly feminine. My job was to hold the work to the brief and the evidence: women were already the buyers, and the founder wanted them addressed out loud. The work went bold.

</SpineSection>

<SpineSection label="Market Thesis" subhead="Women take charge of their sexual health — from home, or from a phone">

The bet was that the primary purchaser wanted a brand on her side, products she could check, and a way to buy without standing in the aisle. Direct-to-consumer was the access strategy, not just the channel.

</SpineSection>

<SpineSection label="The Turn" subhead="Nobody wants condoms on a schedule">

The first site sold condoms and lube on subscription. Once it was live, the model didn't fit: a fixed delivery left people with too many or too few.

Sustain came back to pivot to femcare — tampons, pads, and liners every month, a need that actually recurs. Getting the bundle right meant designing onboarding around each person's cycle, flow, and habits, so the subscription fit before the first box shipped. Product, brand, and business model moved together.

</SpineSection>

## Results

- Client returned for a second engagement: the pivot from a condom subscription to femcare
- Brand voice, art direction, and ecommerce for the sexual wellness launch
- A build-your-own period kit, with onboarding that sizes each subscription to the buyer's cycle
