The identity had to work at app-icon scale and hold a practice as quiet as breathwork without going soft.

**Logo.** A compact HealHaus lockup with an arc that draws itself closed — the mark doubles as the loader.

**Symbols.** Three families, one per aspect. Mind is built from squares and circles. Body from heavier, rounded octagons. Spirit from pattern — stripes, bars, checks. Each set is distinct at a glance and clearly related, so a category reads before its label does.

**Color.** Warm skin and clay tones, honey and marigold accents, black for type. Nine secondary hues split across the three aspects.
