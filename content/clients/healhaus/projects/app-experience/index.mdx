**Onboarding.** Three questions do the front-desk work. Which aspects of self are you looking to develop? Which modalities would you like to explore? Which qualities do you want to cultivate — calm, clarity, strength, grounding, compassion? The answers drive everything a new member sees next.

::: scroll
1. Landing — "A home for your healing journey"
2. Create account
3. Choose subscription — yearly, monthly, free trial
4. Onboarding — aspects of self
5. Onboarding — modalities
6. Onboarding — qualities to cultivate
Caption: Three questions do the work a front desk does in person.
:::

**Today.** The home screen is a daily plan: your classes, recommended videos, upcoming workshops. Each aspect gets its own category page with its own color and symbol.

::: pair
1. Homepage (Today)
2. Category pages — Mind, Body, Spirit
:::

**Library and people.** On-demand video, live classes with booking, and healer profiles — each healer's modalities, upcoming classes, and videos on one page. Search and filter run on the same aspect and modality taxonomy as onboarding.

::: scroll
1. On-demand videos and player
2. Live classes and class details
3. Invite a friend
4. Our Healers and healer profile
5. Search and filter
6. Menu, modalities, account, payment
Caption: The same taxonomy runs onboarding, navigation, and search.
:::

**Profile.** A member's history — classes taken, healers followed, saved videos — reflected back as practice over time.
