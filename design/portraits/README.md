# Portraits

Photos of Greg, mostly 35mm, kept for the About / bio section at the top of Info and anywhere the
site needs a person in it. Not published: nothing here is served. To use one, copy it into
`public/images/` (or an entity's `/images`) and give it alt text.

Before any of these go live:
- Get the photographer's name for a credit line (`type-caption-credit`), especially the two Hudson
  Cannabis harvest portraits (HC_HARVEST-155, HUDSON_CANNABIS_GREENHOUSE_HARVEST_2023_645-089),
  which look like commissioned shoots.
- Several show friends or colleagues (IMG_0560, robgreg, IMG_8724, 000622720006). Ask them before
  publishing, or crop to Greg.

What's here: the harvest-room portrait in a Tyvek suit and orange gloves; the greenhouse portrait with
straw beds; black-and-white in a field with a friend; carrying a bucket of cut flowers; on the beach in
a striped towel; touring a hoop house; floating in black and white; the lake series (double frames,
floating, half-submerged).
